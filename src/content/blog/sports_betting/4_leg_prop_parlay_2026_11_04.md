### 4. Rudy Gobert Over 10.5 points + assists (−114)

This is the contrarian Wolves leg. Gobert went 13 (9 points, 4 assists) against the Clippers and 11 (10 points, 1 assist) against Milwaukee, but only 2 against Golden State. Houston without Adams has fewer bodies to keep him off the offensive glass, and Minnesota ran up 42 paint points and 21 offensive rebounds against Milwaukee. Free throws are his volatility (4-for-12 against MIL), so this leg lives and dies at the line.

## How they relate

- **Legs 1 and 2 go together:** a strong Houston night lifts both Rockets bigs. They also share the same risk. If Houston wins by 20, both may sit the fourth quarter.
- **Legs 3 and 4 work against each other a little:** fewer Ball assists means fewer lobs for Gobert, so his points have to come from putbacks and free throws.
- The best case for the whole ticket is a **close game decided by Houston's frontcourt**, which is fairly plausible at 7.5 points.
